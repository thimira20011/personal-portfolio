# Thimira's engineering portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Software engineering internship recruiters (confirmed by the owner). Visitors should be able to assess the owner's work, inspect source and live projects, open the resume, and make contact.

## Product Purpose

Present Thimira Niranjaya's existing software engineering work and experience with a memorable, professional personal identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing React 18 / Create React App site. Preserve project facts, credentials, resume and social URLs, filtering, case-study disclosures, theme preference, and contact form. Do not fabricate achievements or project outcomes. Changes may be committed locally; do not push or publish.

## Brand Commitments

The owner requested ink black and sand brown as primary colors, chose electric lime for highlights, and chose an animated pixel developer workspace with a monitor and plant. References supplied by the owner: redoyanulhaque.me, ad1tya.me, and emmabostian/developer-portfolios on GitHub. The experience should feel alive with smooth animation while retaining professional developer presentation.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing content in src/data/portfolio.js and src/data/projectStories.js. Local project screenshots and portrait in public/images. NearU image is a capture of its actual public demo. Existing interaction tests cover navigation, projects, theme, and contact behavior.

## Product Principles

- Make personality visible immediately and keep evidence easy to inspect.
- Preserve real work and credentials as the source of credibility.
- Keep the page responsive, keyboard accessible, and usable with reduced motion.
